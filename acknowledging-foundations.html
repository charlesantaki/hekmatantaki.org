Acknowledging the Foundations of Scientific Work

An alphabetical acknowledgement of earlier scientific work

Scientific referencesThesis acknowledgementsHistorical methods

This archive documents later scientific publications, research developments and patents that cite, apply or further develop the published chemistry of Hekmat Bechir Fathallah Antaki (1923–1993), helping to trace its subsequent development and use.

This page looks in the opposite direction — towards the earlier literature on which Antaki’s own work rested.

Science is an accumulation of knowledge across generations. Every scientific contribution rests upon the observations, discoveries, methods and understanding established by earlier researchers.

The author of this archive wishes to acknowledge and honour the scientists whose publications were cited in Antaki's 1950 doctoral thesis and subsequent scientific papers. Their work formed part of the scientific foundations upon which his own investigations developed.

The following alphabetical index records these researchers, their cited publications and where they appear in Antaki's scientific work. It is intended both as an acknowledgement of their contributions and as a traceable historical reference for researchers and historians of chemistry.

Whether widely recognised or little remembered today, their contributions deserve to remain part of the scientific record.

Sources of the index: Antaki’s 1950 doctoral thesis and eight chemistry papers published between 1951 and 1967. Full bibliographic references are listed below.
