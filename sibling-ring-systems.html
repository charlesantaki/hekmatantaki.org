Fused Pyrimidines: Thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidinones and Pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazoles

Primary source: Antaki, H.; Petrow, V. J. Chem. Soc. 1951, 551–555. DOI: 10.1039/jr9510000551

In their 1951 paper (J. Chem. Soc., 551), Antaki and Petrow reported four distinct fused heterocyclic ring systems, each formed by reaction of a bicyclic 2-aminobase with ethyl β-aminocrotonate or a related reagent. The paper is most widely cited for establishing the pyrido[1,2-a]pyrimidin-4-one ring system. But the same paper contained four ring systems in total, each introduced for the first time: the thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidin-5-one, the pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazol-4-one, the pyrimido[2,1-b]benzoxazol-4-one, and the pyrimido[1,2-a]benzimidazole.

The ring systems documented below were each first reported by Antaki and Petrow in 1951. Their status as foundational prior art was recorded in pharmaceutical patents of the 1970s and 1980s. Their derivatives continue to be studied as synthetic and medicinal-chemistry scaffolds through 2025.

Thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidin-5-one

Formed by reaction of 2-aminothiazole with ethyl β-aminocrotonate. A bicyclic system — five-membered thiazole fused to six-membered pyrimidine — with the carbonyl at the 5-position. Antaki and Petrow reported the first synthesis of this ring class.

The ring was cited as prior art in Mead Johnson's US 4,223,031 (1980): "Thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidinones and pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazolones have been previously described by… Antaki, et al., J. Chem. Soc., 551 (1951)…"

Modern synthetic and medicinal applications

Synthesis, Antibacterial and Antitubercular Activities of Some 5H-Thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidin-5-ones and Sulfonic Acid Derivatives
Cai et al. Molecules 2015, 20, 16419–16434. DOI: 10.3390/molecules200916419

A series of 5H-thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidin-5-ones evaluated for in vitro antibacterial activity against Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria and antitubercular activity against Mycobacterium tuberculosis by broth dilution assay.

Synthesis, Crystal Structure, and Biological Evaluation of Fused Thiazolo[3,2-a]Pyrimidines as New Acetylcholinesterase Inhibitors
Mahgoub et al. Molecules 2019, 24, 2306. DOI: 10.3390/molecules24122306

Fused thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidines evaluated as human acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (IC50 values in the 1 µM range), confirmed by X-ray crystallography and molecular docking; investigated as potential leads relevant to Alzheimer's disease.

Synthetic Approaches and Biological Evaluation of Nitrogen Bridgehead Thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidines
Abdel-Megid et al. Results in Chemistry 2024, 11, 101807. DOI: 10.1016/j.rechem.2024.101807

A dedicated review of nitrogen-bridgehead thiazolo[3,2-a]pyrimidines covering synthetic strategies and reported biological activities including antitumor (Topo II inhibition), antimicrobial, antipsychotic, anti-inflammatory, anti-Parkinson, analgesic, antidepressant and anti-HIV. The review notes that specific derivatives in the surveyed literature reached antitumour activity comparable to doxorubicin.

Thiazolopyrimidine, a Privileged Scaffold: Recent Updates on Synthetic and Pharmacological Perspective in Drug Discovery
Khator & Monga. Archiv der Pharmazie 2025, 358, e2400870. DOI: 10.1002/ardp.202400870

Review describing the thiazolopyrimidine scaffold as a "privileged scaffold" in medicinal chemistry, with anticancer, antimicrobial, analgesic, antioxidant and anti-inflammatory applications; the scaffold also functions as a key intermediate for fused heterocycles of medicinal importance.

Pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazol-4-one

Formed by reaction of 2-aminobenzothiazole with ethyl β-aminocrotonate. A tricyclic system: benzene fused to thiazole, fused to pyrimidine via the 2,1-b bond, with the carbonyl at the 4-position. The benzologous extension of the thiazolopyrimidinone above.

Pfizer's US 4,041,163 states in its prior-art section: "The pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazole ring system was first reported by Antaki, et al., J. Chem. Soc., 551 (1951)." Mead Johnson's US 4,223,031 (1980) cites it in the same prior-art sentence as the thiazolopyrimidinone above.

Modern synthetic and medicinal applications

Synthesis and In Vitro Antitumor Activity of New Series of Benzothiazole and Pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazole Derivatives
Gabr, El-Gohary, El-Bendary & El-Kerdawy. Eur. J. Med. Chem. 2014. DOI: 10.1016/j.ejmech.2014.07.097

Nineteen pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazole derivatives screened by the National Cancer Institute (NCI), USA, against a panel of 60 cancer cell lines. Most active compounds: 17 (GI₅₀ = 0.44 µM, TGI = 1.2 µM) and 4 (GI₅₀ = 0.77 µM, TGI = 2.08 µM). Pharmacophoric mapping carried out for both.

Bentonite/Ti(IV) as a Natural Based Nano-catalyst for Synthesis of Pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazole under Grinding Condition
Keihanfar, Mirjalili & Bamoniri. Scientific Reports 2025, 15, 6328. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-80092-z

One-pot three-component solvent-free synthesis of pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazole derivatives using a bentonite/Ti(IV) nanocatalyst. The paper reviews reported activities of the scaffold in the prior literature: anti-allergic, antitumor, antiviral and antituberculosis.

Novel 4H-Pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazoles Derivatives: Camphorsulphonic Acid Catalyzed Enantioselective Synthesis, Optimization, and Biological Study
Bhoi et al. Phosphorus, Sulfur, and Silicon and the Related Elements 2023, 198, 822–835. DOI: 10.1080/10426507.2023.2199994

Enantioselective one-pot three-component synthesis of fourteen 4H-pyrimido[2,1-b]benzothiazole single-isomer derivatives using a chiral camphorsulphonic acid catalyst (20 mol%), achieving 84–99% ee; antibacterial evaluation of all products.

Pyrimido[2,1-b]benzoxazol-4-one

Formed by reaction of 2-aminobenzoxazole with ethyl β-aminocrotonate. Structurally analogous to the benzothiazole system above, with oxygen replacing sulfur in the five-membered ring. First reported by Antaki and Petrow in 1951.

The ring has been studied as a component of fused ring systems with antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory potential. Its primary scientific significance is as a member of the class of privileged heterocyclic scaffolds established in the 1951 paper.

Pyrimido[1,2-a]benzimidazole

Formed by reaction of 2-aminobenzimidazole with ethyl β-aminocrotonate. Unlike the three ring systems above, this compound lacks a carbonyl — the ring closure produces a fully aromatic tricyclic nitrogen system. First reported by Antaki and Petrow in 1951.

This ring system has been investigated as a scaffold for kinase inhibitors and as a DNA-intercalating agent. Takeda Pharmaceutical Company (2018) and Kumar and colleagues (2021) have reported derivatives with antiproliferative and antimicrobial activity.

The 1951 Paper in Context

The full scope of J. Chem. Soc. 1951, 551 is rarely acknowledged in citation practice. The paper is most commonly cited — correctly — for establishing the pyrido[1,2-a]pyrimidin-4-one ring and correcting a structural error that had persisted since 1911. But the same paper introduced four further fused ring systems in a single publication, each demonstrated for the first time by the same condensation methodology.

The patents of the 1970s and 1980s — Pfizer, Mead Johnson, E.R. Squibb, CIBA — reached back to this paper precisely because it was the foundational primary source for this class of ring systems. The modern literature on thiazolopyrimidines, pyrimidobenzothiazoles, and related scaffolds does not consistently trace the origin back to 1951; but the scaffolds it studies are the ones that paper introduced.
